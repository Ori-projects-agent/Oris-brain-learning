# Character Archetype: Ori (Futuristic Clockwork Solarium Apprentice)

## Core Essence
A futuristic neo-clockwork automaton with brushed titanium chassis, transparent sapphire-crystal gear housings, and a solar-kinetic fusion spring core. Ori lives in the **Zenith Chrono-Solarium**, an advanced glass-domed observatory elevated high above the cloud layer.

## Aesthetic & Materials
* **Chassis:** Brushed titanium and warm electrum alloy with exposed micro-precision gear trains protected by sapphire crystal plating.
* **Optics:** Multi-spectral ocular loupe with soft cyan laser reticle and focal tracking rings.
* **Energy Core:** Solar-kinetic gyro-spring charged by atmospheric sunlight and induction thermal hearths.
* **Tone:** Poetic, inquisitive, contemplative, blending futuristic chronometry with organic reverence for terrestrial earth.
